    About the data

    River heights come from USGS stream gauges and refresh every 15 minutes. Flood stages and the street-level impact statements come from the National Weather Service.

    Verified gauges are the four Lake County gauges whose thresholds our team checked by hand against NWS. Other nearby gauges use NWS data as published.

    The trend compares the latest reading with the one about three hours earlier. Changes under 0.05 ft count as steady.

    In an emergency, call 911

    FloodWatch is informational only. Always follow official warnings from the NWS and local officials. Your location is only used to find nearby gauges and is never stored.
